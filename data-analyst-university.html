The Discovery

Then I started noticing something nobody was talking about

I was applying to hundreds of listings — every job board, every city. And because I was looking at so many of them, I started seeing patterns that people who only follow a roadmap would never see. Three things jumped out.

Finding 01

The wrong skills

The skills showing up in the most job postings weren't the ones most courses and bootcamps were teaching. I was getting really good at the wrong things.

Finding 02

"Entry-level" isn't entry-level

70% of postings I applied to required 2–3 years of experience — for roles labeled entry-level. A catch-22 nobody in education had any reason to admit.

Finding 03

300,000 hidden roles

Most companies weren't calling the role "data analyst." Same work, same skills — different titles. 300,000+ open positions hiding in plain sight, almost nobody applying.
