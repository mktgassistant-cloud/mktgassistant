Who this is for

A good fit if you're the analyst who keeps fixing the data before you can analyze it.

  • You know SQL basics (SELECT, JOIN, GROUP BY) and you want to go a level deeper.
  • You've rebuilt the same spreadsheet or report by hand more times than you'd like to admit.
  • You want a portfolio project you can walk a hiring manager through, line by line.
  • You can give it about 8 to 10 hours a week for two months.

Changing careers and still new to SQL? You can still join. You'll do the pre-work first, and we'll talk on the call about whether this cohort or the next one makes more sense.

What you'll build

One pipeline, upgraded every week

Every student works on the same fictional company. You start with a raw HYROS export in week one and get it onto a chart that same week. Then you keep adding sources, structure, and safeguards until it looks like something a data team would actually run.

  1. Wk 0
    Setup and pre-work
    Accounts, tools, and a SQL warm-up. Plan on 4 to 6 hours.
  2. Wk 1
    End to end on day one
    Take a raw HYROS export into Databricks and out to a Tableau chart.
  3. Wk 2
    Add YouTube exports
    Bring in a second source and line it up with the first.
  4. Wk 3
    Add Meta ad spend
    Calculate cost per lead and CAC, the numbers marketing leaders ask for every Monday.
  5. Wk 4
    Model it as a star schema
    Fact and dimension tables that other people can query without calling you.
  6. Wk 5
    Tests, data dictionary, access rules
    Catch bad data before your stakeholders do, and document what each field means.
  7. Wk 6
    Automate it
    Schedule the pipeline with Lakeflow Jobs and practice handling a broken run.
  8. Wk 7
    Stakeholder dashboard
    Build the dashboard a marketing team would open first thing in the morning.
  9. Wk 8
    A/B test analysis
    Analyze a real experiment, start using Python, and present your full pipeline live to Kedeisha.

Questions

Before you book

How much SQL do I need?

If you can write a query with a JOIN and a GROUP BY, you're ready. If that still feels shaky, the pre-work in week 0 is there to close the gap, and we'll be straight with you on the call about whether it's enough.

Can I do this with a full-time job?

That's who it's built for. Plan on roughly 8 to 10 hours a week: two live sessions, plus build time you schedule yourself. Weeks with a new data source tend to run a little heavier.

Do I need to know Python?

No. The program is SQL first. Python comes in during week 8 for the A/B test analysis, and we start from the basics there.

What does it cost?

We go over pricing and payment options on the call, once we know the program fits your goals. The internship is priced separately and is optional.

When does it start?

Cohort 1 kicks off in late January 2027. Week 0 setup and pre-work happens right before the first live session, and we'll send exact dates when you book.

What do I walk away with?

A working, tested, automated pipeline in your own GitHub, a stakeholder dashboard, a Demo Day presentation you've already given once, and (if you do the internship) a write-up you can put straight on your resume.
